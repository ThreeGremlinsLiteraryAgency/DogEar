What happens next?

As soon as the club is created, DogEar will take you to its new club page.

  • You'll become President automatically.
  • You'll be added as the club's first member.
  • You can set up your first monthly vote.
  • You can invite readers and appoint admins.
  • Club settings can be changed later.
