Exact editions matter

DogEar stores the Google Books volume ID, ISBN-13, ISBN-10, publisher, publication date, and cover so members know exactly which edition they are voting on.

Finalizing the winner

Once voting is closed, DogEar counts the votes on the server. A clear first-place book can be finalized automatically. If first place is tied, only the Club President can choose among the tied books.

Monthly ballots

Each month has its own ballot record. Votes from one month cannot carry into another month's vote.
